# Ethical AI Governance for SDLC

An ethical AI governance framework for promoting responsible, fair, transparent, and accountable AI throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

## 📌 Project Overview

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being used in modern software applications. However, AI systems can introduce ethical challenges such as bias, privacy concerns, lack of transparency, security risks, and unclear accountability.

This project provides a web-based platform that helps developers understand and consider ethical AI principles throughout different stages of the Software Development Life Cycle.

The platform focuses on ethical assessment, governance guidelines, risk identification, and responsible AI practices.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional Software Development Life Cycle approaches mainly focus on functionality, performance, and software quality. Ethical considerations are often not systematically addressed during the development process.

This project aims to integrate ethical AI considerations into the SDLC so that potential risks can be identified and addressed from the early stages of development.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Integrate ethical AI principles into the SDLC.
- Identify potential ethical risks in AI-based systems.
- Assess the ethical readiness of an AI project.
- Promote fairness and transparency in AI systems.
- Improve awareness of privacy and security.
- Encourage accountability in AI development.
- Provide recommendations for responsible AI development.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Ethical AI Assessment** – Evaluates the ethical readiness of an AI project.
- **Risk Identification** – Identifies potential ethical risks related to AI.
- **Governance Guidelines** – Provides guidelines for responsible AI development.
- **Fairness & Bias Awareness** – Helps identify potential bias and fairness concerns.
- **Transparency** – Promotes understandable and transparent AI systems.
- **Privacy Protection** – Highlights responsible data and privacy practices.
- **Security Awareness** – Considers security risks associated with AI systems.
- **Accountability** – Encourages clear responsibility for AI decisions and outcomes.
- **Explainability** – Promotes understandable AI decision-making.
- **Responsible AI Recommendations** – Provides guidance for improving ethical practices.
