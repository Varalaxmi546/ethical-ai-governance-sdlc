Key Features of Ethical AI

Fairness: Ensures unbiased outcomes.

Transparency: Explains AI decisions clearly.

Accountability: Assigns responsibility.

Privacy: Protects personal data.

Security: Prevents misuse.

Robustness: Handles failures safely.

Human Oversight: Maintains human control.

Compliance: Meets legal standards.

Auditability: Enables tracking.

Sustainability: Supports long-term ethical use.
